What this simulation is NOT: a deterministic year-long backtest. It bootstraps from a single day (2026-05-11) of validated entries, assumes each future trading day samples from that empirical distribution, applies a 20% edge-decay haircut, and runs 5,000 trials.

Why the decay haircut matters: yesterday's tape was unusually mean-reverting — perfect conditions for the strategy. Without a decay factor, the simulator would project 5,000 years averaging multi-thousand-percent returns (fantasy). The 20% default acknowledges that ~80% of yesterday's edge is regime-specific and won't persist.
