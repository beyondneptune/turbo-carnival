Honest caveats

What this backtest assumes:
• No commissions (Alpaca paper is $0; real broker ~$0.65/contract for options, but this is ETF)
• No slippage (SVXY has tight spread, but real fills move with size)
• VIX/VIX3M data from yfinance (occasional missing days)
• Walk-forward means signal at T-1 close → position at T open (1-day lag)
What this does NOT capture:
• Black-swan vol spikes (2020 March COVID: SVXY lost 80%+ in 3 weeks — the strategy was flat for the spike but the timing depended on the ratio's lag)
• Volume / liquidity constraints (we assume we can buy/sell at close)
• Tax implications (SVXY is regulated investment company — gains taxed as 1099-DIV with K-1)
